# ​Are Instagram product links detected as backlinks? 

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-11-07
> **Tags:** Ahrefs, Ana, backlinks

---

If an Instagram influencer points a link to a product on the user's webiste, we won't show it.



The reason is because Ahrefs won't show it. [Their official answer]() is copied below:
_We do not report the major portion of Instagram links as they are placed on orphan pages, we simply do not crawl pages in Instagram as there are no links that will tell us to crawl some more pages. If somebody links to an Instagram account, we can follow this link and check Instagram page and report backlinks from there, but this is a very small number that will be reported by us. _
