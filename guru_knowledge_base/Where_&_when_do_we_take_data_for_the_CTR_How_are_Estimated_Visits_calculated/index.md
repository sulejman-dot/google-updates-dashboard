# Where & when do we take data for the CTR? How are Estimated Visits calculated?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-12-07
> **Tags:** CTR, CTR frequency, estimated monthly visits, Mircea

---

If a website has a GSC connected, we take the data for the CTR, both in SEO Campaign and in Organic Traffic from their GSC profile.

CTR will show values only if the keyword has Average Position less than 10, otherwise it will appear with "-" or with "N/A".

If GSC is not connected - the CTR in both areas should appear either with "-" or with "N/A".

The CTR is per keyword and does not change based on device.

As for the CTR in the [Estimated Visits](http://take.ms/dkFLf) from the Competition Insights section, we use a standard CTR, based on the position in the SERP (regardless oh whether the GSC is connected or not), but soon it will be upgraded to a dynamic one, based on a more detailed research, taking into consideration SERP features, the market, keyword type and device distribution.  

**The Estimated Visits** are calculated based on one of our algorithms, taking into consideration search volumes, ranks, and a CTR-type of proprietary function.
