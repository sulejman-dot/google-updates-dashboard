# Update goals and reprocess data

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2021-02-19
> **Tags:** conversions, Delia, goals, organic traffic, reprocessing

---

When you update your goals with reprocessing, the data will be altered retroactively once the reprocessing is done.

If you update the goals without reprocessing, the data will adjust accordingly starting the next day (next automatic processing).
