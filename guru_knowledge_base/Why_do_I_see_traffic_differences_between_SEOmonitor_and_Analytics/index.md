# Why do I see traffic differences between SEOmonitor and Analytics?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2022-11-07
> **Tags:** gbp, google business profile, Ioana, traffic, traffic data

---

Besides the info in [this](https://help.seomonitor.com/en/articles/927395-comparing-the-traffic-data-between-seomonitor-and-google-analytics) article, it can be a "google business profile" that's not correctly named. 

To pull traffic data, we filter on the source=@google ➝ translation: the source name needs to contain GOOGLE. 

**_If the source does not contain google, we don't pull data on it_**
