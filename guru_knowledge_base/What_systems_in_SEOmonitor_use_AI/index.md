# What systems in SEOmonitor use AI?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2025-12-15
> **Tags:** AI

---

Which parts of the tool rely on AI technologies and how?

Magic keywords (voluntary) - using AI in research 

AI Clustering (voluntary) - using AI in group structure 

Content Writer - using AI in content production to automate content production workflows 

Forecast: AI Summary analysis of the forecast (voluntary) , helps to provide context , highlight challenges to the forecast  that was configured

AIS/AIO - content is processed with AI and analysis of brands

SV click stream data replaced and is estimated with AI (sensitive topics that Google doesn’t provide SV for)
