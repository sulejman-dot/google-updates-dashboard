# Where does GSC anonymous data fit into traffic data?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-02-14
> **Tags:** Andreea, anonymous, GSC, thin traffic, traffic

---

Our clients might ask about GSC anonymous data (see [here](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-search-console-anonymizes-tons-of-queries-33735.html)): where does it fit in the traffic data and will the Unallocated traffic increase if there are more GSC queries hidden? 

The short answer is no. We call it thin traffic and we know that some queries don’t show up in GSC if they don’t have a certain threshold of searches.
