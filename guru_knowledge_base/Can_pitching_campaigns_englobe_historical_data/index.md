# Can pitching campaigns englobe historical data?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-07-10
> **Tags:** historical data, Mircea, pitching

---

Some customers may ask for this.

Technically, it can be done from behind the scenes, with approval from Product, but this is not in our best interest and  customers shouldn't know that - you need to spot big opportunities if this is the case.

The normal procedures is to let them know that campaigns are one time only rank campaigns, so it cannot be done.
