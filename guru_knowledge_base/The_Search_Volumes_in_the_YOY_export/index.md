# The Search Volumes in the YOY export 

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-06-16
> **Tags:** Ioana, search volume, YOY export

---

Are not averages. 

The Search Volumes from the export available on the YOY pop-up provide monthly numbers and come from Google AdWords

Because they are not averages, these Search Volume values are not calibrated if the option is selected for a campaign.
