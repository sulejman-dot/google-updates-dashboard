Note:

The SERP Discrepancy Checker compares the real-time SERP of a tracked keyword from our crawlers with what the user sees on their Google search (on their browser, Chrome). 

Since in **Draft** campaigns keywords are not tracked, the Discrepancy Checker **cannot** be used for draft campaigns.
