# Is the multi-location option available for pitching campaigns?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2021-06-23
> **Tags:** Delia, location, market, multi-location, new campaign, pitching

---

**No.** This was created for regular campaigns to help clients save money whilst the pitching campaigns are, for now, completely free and unlimited (as long as you have at least one regular campaign in the account).
