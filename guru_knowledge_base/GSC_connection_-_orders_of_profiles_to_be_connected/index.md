# GSC connection - orders of profiles to be connected

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-06-22
> **Tags:** Ana, GSC

---

For campaigns that are set for a specific country/language.

The normal behavior is to connect first one of the main profiles, https://language.domain.com or sc-domain:language.domain.com) and only after to be able to connect another one (like the general one of your website, https://domain.com)



Update after [task](https://app.clickup.com/t/5yug28):  this order does not apply to Wizard, where the checks are not the same as in Website Settings, and where the general profile can be connected.
