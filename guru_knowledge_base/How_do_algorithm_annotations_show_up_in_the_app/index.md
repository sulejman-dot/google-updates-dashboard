# How do algorithm annotations show up in the app?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-03-18
> **Tags:** algorithm change, annotations, Mircea

---

There are numerous Google algorithm updates and changes throughout the year, we only show the major ones in the platform (the official ones on which Google povides sufficient details, in press conferences, etc).

We take such updates from SEJ, [https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-algorithm-history/](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-algorithm-history/), and they should automatically appear in the system.
