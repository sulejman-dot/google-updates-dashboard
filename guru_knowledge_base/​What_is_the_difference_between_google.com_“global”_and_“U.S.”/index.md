# ​What is the difference between google.com “global” and “U.S.”?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-08-26
> **Tags:** Ana, wizard

---

A short explanation of the .com search engines available in step 2 of setting up a campaign.

 When [setting up a campaign](https://help.seomonitor.com/en/articles/1320803-how-to-correctly-set-up-a-campaign-in-seomonitor), and selecting google.com, there are 2 options for this search engine: google.com (global) and google.com (US).

The difference between those 2 is that:

- for google.com (global) - we provide global search volumes

- for google.com (US) - we provide US search volumes



_Note that all keyword rankings and search volumes will be provided based on this setting._
