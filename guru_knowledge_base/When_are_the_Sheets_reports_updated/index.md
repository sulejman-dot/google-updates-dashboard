# When are the Sheets reports updated?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-06-22
> **Tags:** automatic update, Ioana, sheets add-on, sheets report

---

If you enable the option to have the Sheets report updated automatically, it will be refreshed in reference to the date it was created and considering the frequency to which you want it to happen.

You can see the date of the next update at the right top corner of the spreadsheet.
