# ​Why is there no value for Search Volume in Topics?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-03-07
> **Tags:** Maria, search volume, topic explorer, topics

---

The reason why you see such cases - [https://take.ms/obSHL](https://take.ms/obSHL) is due to the fact that some of the keywords are **crawled on-the-fly** and it takes a while (sometimes even more than several hours) until the search volume data is displayed (either until it is received from KT.io or, if they are from SEOmonitor, it can last until the SV is available in the app as well).

**Note**: The team will create a label/warning to inform people that the search volume is _pending_ the update.
