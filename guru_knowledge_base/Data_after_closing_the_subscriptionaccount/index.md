# Data after closing the subscription/account

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2021-03-31
> **Tags:** closed account, closed subscription, data export, ranks history

---

When closing their account we show a popup letting them know that they should export their data before the account expires; after that they’ll not be able to retrieve it anymore. Issues with exports for data before 2020 will be fixed as well.


**Before this is implement, we reactivate their account for 1 day and let them export whatever they want. Also, we'll make tasks for the ranks exports that currently can't be downloaded from the app (prior to 2020)**
