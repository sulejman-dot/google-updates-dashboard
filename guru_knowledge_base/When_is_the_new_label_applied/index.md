# When is the "new" label applied?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2025-11-23
> **Tags:** Ioana, labels, the "new" label

---

This label is relative to the timeframe; it means that the keyword was added within the timeframe selected in the calendar.

The reason for this behaviour is to let users know that those keywords don’t affect the Visibility metric for that entire timeframe.

*It will be re-discussed in the backlog meeting.
