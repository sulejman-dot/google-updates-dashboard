# Ranks related messages

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2022-02-03
> **Tags:** Ioana, ranks, ranks issues, ranks issues message

---

"For any SERP changes, we do have automatic processes in place, to show us anomalies in the SERPs and proceed with fixing them immediately. So this would be just "business as usual”: when we’d detect huge increases or drops in the number of keywords in our database that have a specific SERP feature or sudden drops in ranks, or when we'd detect known issues on specific features (for example, keywords like "restaurants near me" no longer featuring a map). Due to the nature of the process, SERP features and the page "reading" are prone to error, as Google often changes the HTML of the SERP."

"These changes in the page don't all roll out at the same time: different SERP features get updated at different times, even different _combinations_ of features, different markets, and so on. We have multiple systems in place to ensure both that "all ranks ready daily at 8AM" (99%) SLA is met and that these ranks have full integrity (are correct). However, there are thresholds that trigger our alarms for what seem to be updates, and sometimes they are not met. So we manually go in and update the systems as soon as we or you guys notice anything off. We know this isn't perfect, and we're always improving to get as close to that as we can."
