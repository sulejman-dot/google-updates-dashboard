Some of our clients have created their own connection with DataStudio through the API.

Since November 2018, we have been having the development ready, here is the guide -  - [http://help.seomonitor.com/faqs/data-studio-guidelines](http://help.seomonitor.com/faqs/data-studio-guidelines).

 They need to bear in mind that the reports from Data studio need to be set up separately for each individual project, for each specific time frame you have in mind (according to the parameters for each endpoint in API).

If asked why we haven't yet been published in the Google DataStudio Connector ‘library’, we have applied for that - still pending for their approval, tedious process. The current development is a way of bypassing that.
