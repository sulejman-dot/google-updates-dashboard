# When is the NO Analytics option available?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-04-19
> **Tags:** GA, Maria, wizard

---

The "No Analytics" or no GA option ([https://take.ms/XN8bw)](https://take.ms/XN8bw)) in wizard is available only for the UK/US clients or trials.

For the rest of the clients, they can have this type of project, if they click 'Skip this step' button ([https://take.ms/DmPHI](https://take.ms/DmPHI)) if they added at least one project before.

If they are trials outside US & UK, they can 'Skip this step' if they stopped in wizard 2 and after 2 days they have this option available.
