# Do build-measure clients have to upgrade to the Pro Plan to get the AIS add-on?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2025-11-21
> **Tags:** AIS, billing, build measure, Delia, grandfathered, grandfathered pricing, grandfathered subscription

---

Yes, build-measure clients are considered grandfathered, and every account that doesn't have the option to "Change Plan" is grandfathered and must upgrade to the Pro Plan first to enable the AIS Add-On.
