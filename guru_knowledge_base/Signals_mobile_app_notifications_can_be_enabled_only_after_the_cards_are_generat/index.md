# Signals mobile app: notifications can be enabled only after the cards are generated? 

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-03-29
> **Tags:** Ana, notifications, signals

---

The enable, and disable, of notifications, for a campaign, in the mobile app can be done at any time.
Signals cards don't have to be generated to enable the notifications.  

The notifications are related to each user, not to the campaign.
