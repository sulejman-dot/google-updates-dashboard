# Can a user get YoY search volume right after migrating a campaign?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2021-06-11
> **Tags:** Delia, migrating, migration, search volume, year-over-year, YOY, yoy trends

---

We migrate ranks only, not search volumes so a migrated campaign will be considered a new campaign in terms of YoY and the information from this card [here](https://app.getguru.com/card/cd9d7gEi/When-is-YoY-supposed-to-be-displayed?q=yoy) applies.
