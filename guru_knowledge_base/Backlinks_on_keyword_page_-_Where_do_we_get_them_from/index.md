# Backlinks on keyword page - Where do we get them from?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2022-05-18
> **Tags:** Alex, backlinks

---

Backlink information is taken from SERPstat and can be checked by accessing their website and looking up the domain. Being under “Landing page data”, the backlinks export should be links toward the page the keyword is ranking with.

SERPstat link: [https://serpstat.com/backlinks/dashboard/?query=experimenteaza.ro&se=g_ro&search_type=subdomains
