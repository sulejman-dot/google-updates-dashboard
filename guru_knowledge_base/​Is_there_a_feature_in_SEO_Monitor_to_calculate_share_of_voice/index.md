# ​Is there a feature in SEO Monitor to calculate share of voice?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2018-11-27
> **Tags:** competition insights, Maria

---

At the moment, a similar thing can be found in our Competition insights section, in the form of our Visibility Score.
The difference between the two is that if we were to display the share of voice sum, the percentage would exceed 100% (since the share of voice is calculated based on CTR, while our Visibility Score is based on Impression Rates).
However, in the future, we will think of a way of displaying the share of voice too, since we do have this type of data.
