# How ca I remove keywords from Brand group?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-09-23
> **Tags:** brand, brand group, Ioana, remove keyword

---

Certain keywords can be removed from the Brand group by clicking on the small "x" next to the name of the group, at the top of the Keyword page.
