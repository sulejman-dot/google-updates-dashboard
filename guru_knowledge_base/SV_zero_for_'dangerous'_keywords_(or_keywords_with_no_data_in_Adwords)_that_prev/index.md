# SV zero for 'dangerous' keywords (or keywords with no data in Adwords) that previously had such data.

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-02-24
> **Tags:** Maria, search volume

---

If the keywords can be categorized as 'Dangerous products or services', like explosives, guns, gun parts & related products, tobacco, cryptocurrency, recreational drugs, other types of weapons, they will have a zero search volume, since Google Adwords stopped showing this info, as they officially mentioned in this [article](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/6014299?hl=en).

There's also the case of random keywords, for which Adwords shown no data (-) starting from a certain point in time.

When Adwords will offer the volumes for those keywords again, then those values will be automatically displayed in SEOmonitor as well ) and the warning sign will no longer be displayed.
