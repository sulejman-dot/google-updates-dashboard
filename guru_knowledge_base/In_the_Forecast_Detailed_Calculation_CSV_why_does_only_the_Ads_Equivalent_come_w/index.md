# In the Forecast Detailed Calculation CSV: why does only the Ads Equivalent come with decimals?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-07-12
> **Tags:** ads equivalent, detailed calculation, detailed calculation export, export, forecast, Ioana

---

The Ads Equivalent values come with 2 decimals because the Ads values are lower, and the decimals make a difference.

All the other numerical metrics come rounded and without multipliers.
