# How is the AI Overview data displayed on the chart in Competition?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2025-12-30
> **Tags:** AI overview, AIO, chart, Competition, granularity, Ioana

---

The AIO data follows the Visibility model of granularity, respectively:

- week - we display data for Monday
- month - we display data for the last day of the month
