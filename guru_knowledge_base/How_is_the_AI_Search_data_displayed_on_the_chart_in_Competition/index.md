# How is the AI Search data displayed on the chart in Competition?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2025-12-30
> **Tags:** AI Search, AIS, chart, Competition, granularity, Ioana

---

For the chart in Competition, the following algo is used

- _choose the closest date where we have AIS data to the expected date based on the interval length. If two dates are equally distant, choose the later date._

So, for AIS, we display the dates when the AIS crawl was performed.
