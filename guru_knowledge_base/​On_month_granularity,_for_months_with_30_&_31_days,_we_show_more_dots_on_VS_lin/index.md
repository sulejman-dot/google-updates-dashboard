# ​On month granularity, for months with 30 & 31 days, we show more dots on VS line, than the actual months

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-10-15
> **Tags:** granularity, Maria, visibility score

---

For timeframes that include months that have both 30 and 31 days, we'll see more dots on the VS line, on monthly granularity, than the actual number of months.

This is due to the fact that we count a month as 30 days (not 31)

Example: if I have 1 December 2019 - 31 January 2020, I will see 3 dots on VS line, rather than 2 - [https://take.ms/qjTrO.](https://take.ms/qjTrO)
