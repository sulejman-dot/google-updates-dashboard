# Error getting authentication with Google

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-06-04
> **Tags:** Google authentication, Maria

---

When you are logged in with multiple Google accounts, and you try to authtorize one of them for SEOmonitor, Google doesn't know which one to pick and this error appears - [https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/214991418/323e36fc15264f73ddca4fb0/image.png](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/214991418/323e36fc15264f73ddca4fb0/image.png)

You can:

- 
log into the app in an incognito and work with the add-on.

- 
if that doesn't work, you will need to log out of all the accounts and then log in with the email you need to work with.
