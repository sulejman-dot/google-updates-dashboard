# How is the traffic growth distributed over the forecasted months?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-12-18
> **Tags:** distribute traffic, distribute traffic growth, forecast, growth, Ioana, traffic

---

1. 
For each keyword we calculate how many additional monthly sessions will be generated if the rank improves (from the current rank to the desired top). -> Now we know how much additional traffic we’ll have at the end of the forecast period.

1. 
Then we plot the progression on a performance over time curve (default is exponential, but linear or custom are also available) -> that means we start adding the total additional traffic from 0% to 100% in the 12 months (e g: fifth month would get 30% of the total additional traffic that would be generated at the end of the forecast)

1. 
Then we apply the seasonality of the keywords on each month.
