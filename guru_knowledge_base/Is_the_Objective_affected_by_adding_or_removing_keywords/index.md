# Is the Objective affected by adding or removing keywords?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-01-16
> **Tags:** actual traffic, Delia, forecast, forecast objective, objective status

---

The Objective will take into account the keywords you have when setting it.

However, when you remove keywords, you have a warning that looks like this: [https://take.ms/iQws6](https://take.ms/iQws6.)

When you remove keywords the actual traffic would no longer be the same, because if you're not working on the same keywords as estimated, the traffic will no longer be the same either.
