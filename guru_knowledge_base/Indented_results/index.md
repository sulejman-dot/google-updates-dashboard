# Indented results

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2022-05-27
> **Tags:** indented, indented result, Ioana

---

Google is testing indenting search, results snippets from within the same domain name. The indented results simply bring together pages ranking for the same or similar keywords.
So if Google has two or more results from domain.com in the SERP, indented a little under the first one will be the others. 
[https://take.ms/Cj33U](https://take.ms/Cj33U)


**SEOmonitor's is considering these results as organic** and counting them as positions like any other regular result. 
They will be displayed as such on the Keyword Page -> Competition tab and the respective URLs will be provided in the SERPs data export correspondingly

Note: Indented results don't show up on mobile SERPs
