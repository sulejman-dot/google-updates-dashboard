# Why is my keyword ranking 99+ when I see it registering clicks in GSC?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-05-12
> **Tags:** GSC clicks, Ioana, not ranking, rank 99+, rank in seomonitor versus gsc

---

It’s totally normal. The rank is a snapshot in time from a single location. The clicks in GSC are the sum of all clicks done in a timeframe from all locations.

GSC also registers the clicks from personalized results (not incognito). If I’m a frequent user of [seomonitor.com](http://seomonitor.com/), it might include results from [SEOmonitor.com](http://seomonitor.com/) at the top for me. That would not be reflected in the rank.
