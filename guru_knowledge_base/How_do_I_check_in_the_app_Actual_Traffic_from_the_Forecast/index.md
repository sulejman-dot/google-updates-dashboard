# How do I check in the app Actual Traffic from the Forecast?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-10-08
> **Tags:** actual traffic, forecast, Ioana

---

The Actual Traffic metric in the Forecast corresponds to the non-brand Organic traffic (Sessions under the graph) in Organic Traffic section.

Though currently the "i" says "months that passed" it actually shows data even for just a part of the calendar month up to the current date.
