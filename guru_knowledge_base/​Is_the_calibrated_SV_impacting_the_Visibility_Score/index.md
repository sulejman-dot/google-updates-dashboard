# ​Is the calibrated SV impacting the Visibility Score?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-04-23
> **Tags:** Ana, calibrated, search volume, visibility score

---

The SV, [calibrated](https://help.seomonitor.com/en/articles/3923882-search-volumes-after-covid-19) or not, is an essential part of the Visibility Score calculation. 

If there are impacts due to search trend changes it will appear in the VS explainer.
