# How to enable Content Performance section for active campaigns?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-07-15
> **Tags:** content performance, extension, Maria

---

Logged in with the email of the owner or one of the admins & masterpass, go:

1. 
to the Extensions section in the app, click on it and install the Content Performance extension - [https://take.ms/nQ31g.](https://take.ms/nQ31g)

1. 
then you can see it in the top bar - [https://take.ms/8BZTN.](https://take.ms/8BZTN) 


This section will be enabled for all the current campaigns the owner or admin has access to; for new campaigns added in the future, we will need to follow the above steps again.



Update - Ana - 14 july
For new campaigns, the Extension has to be uninstalled and installed again. 

At uninstall it the Content section will be disabled on all campaigns, and at reinstall it will be enabled again on all campaigns.
