# How do Slack notifications work?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-10-22
> **Tags:** alerts, Mircea, notifications, slack

---

We have conceived an alert/notification integration with slack.

It can be set up in the app - [https://app.seomonitor.com/my_account/notifications](https://app.seomonitor.com/my_account/notifications) , according to trigger criteria (such as a certain VS), notification time, desired channel.

When the notifications stop arriving to the customer, we need to test whether it is on our side (=trello task).



Later edit - Ana 
For changing the workgroup for notifications, the only workaround is to disconnect the existing slack channel and to reconnect it to the desired workgroup.
