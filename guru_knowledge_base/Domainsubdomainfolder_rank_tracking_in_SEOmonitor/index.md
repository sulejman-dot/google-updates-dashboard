# Domain/subdomain/folder rank tracking in SEOmonitor

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2024-09-18
> **Tags:** Campaign Settings, Delia, folder, Ioana, main domain, monitor subdomain, subdomain, track subdomain

---

1. If you_ track the main domain and_ select to track _subdomains_ as well, it will take **BOTH** into consideration and provide the best rank between the two.
1. If you _track the subdomain_, it will **ONLY** take the ranks for that subdomain.
1. If you _track a folder/subfolder_ it will **ONLY** take the ranks for that folder/subfolder. (as of September 2024)

For more details regarding tracking subdirectories, check this card: [How can I monitor the subdirectories of a website?](https://app.getguru.com/card/XTpapj8T/How-can-I-monitor-the-subdirectories-of-a-website)


EXTRA INFO: 
From a traffic perspective, they can also connect the Google profiles (GSC&GA) for the folder and have the folder-only distribution of traffic.
