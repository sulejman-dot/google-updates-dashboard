# Why can't I find Taiwan as a country on the markets list

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2025-01-14
> **Tags:** Ioana, localization, market, taiwan

---

According to Google Ads (and the Chinese), Taiwan is not a country. 

Google is a US company, and the US does not recognize Taiwan as a country -> [https://take.ms/cq2Qq0](https://take.ms/cq2Qq0)


So you can find it in the "regions" list: -> [https://take.ms/BNWxk](https://take.ms/BNWxk)
