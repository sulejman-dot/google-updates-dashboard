# CPC metric in the Opportunity pop-up at group level

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2021-04-26
> **Tags:** CPC, group, group level, Ioana, Opportunity, through Ads

---

The total amount estimated to be paid through Ads in order to receive the same number of clicks as additional estimated sessions is the sum of individual/per keyword CPC cost.

The CPC value in brackets is the average CPC cost for that pool of keywords
