# Writer-only Plan: What’s accessible 

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2026-04-15
> **Tags:** billing

---

- Content Writer
- Rank Tracker (with keyword add-on at €49/1K)
- Research (Keyword Explorer + Keyword Vault)
- GSC/GA connection (add-on at €10/connection — feeds performance data into Rank Tracker and Content Writer, but does NOT unlock the Traffic module)
- Unlimited campaigns



**What’s NOT accessible (Pro only)**

- Forecast
- Traffic / Organic Traffic
- Content Audit
- Draft Campaigns
- Agency Dashboard
- Full API access

These are hard-restricted on Writer-Only regardless of add-ons.

**Answers to the LocalFame questions:**
_1. Can we cap articles per month?_
Articles are purchased in batches (€49/10 articles). They only get the articles they pay for — once used, they buy more. That’s the cap.

_2. Keyword archiving & reactivation (seasonal clients)?_
Yes. Keywords can be deactivated to stop billing and reactivated later. When reactivated, tracking history reconnects — not treated as new keywords. Works the same on any plan.

_3. Keyword pool management (20 articles/month x ~20kw)?_
Keywords from published articles get added to tracking automatically if not specifically unchecked when publishing. They start with a 1K keyword slot (€49). Once they pass 1K active keywords, they’d need to buy another 1K batch (+€49). Keyword costs grow in €49/1K steps as articles accumulate.

_4. What does GSC show on Writer-Only?_
With the +€10 GSC connection, they see keyword-level clicks and impressions in Rank Tracker, plus performance tracking for published articles in Content Writer. They do NOT get the full Organic Traffic module — that’s Pro only.

**Bottom line for the prospect:**
Writer-Only + 1K keywords + GSC = ~€108/month. They get content creation, rank tracking, keyword research, vault, and keyword-level GSC data. If they later want Forecast, Traffic analytics, Content Audit, or Agency Dashboard, that’s the upgrade to Pro.
