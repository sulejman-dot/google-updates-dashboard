# Billing/Plan adjustments from the app for ongoing subscriptions

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2021-01-05
> **Tags:** account settings, billing, chargebee, Delia

---

When users make adjustments to their current plan from the Billing Section of the app, they should see the changes made at "Current Price" right after making the changes.

However, in the Chargebee Widget from the app (the one on the right side) and in Chargebee itself, the updates are done once per day at 16:00 Romanian Time.
