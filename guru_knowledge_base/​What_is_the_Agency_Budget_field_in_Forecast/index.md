# ​What is the Agency Budget field in Forecast?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-04-17
> **Tags:** agency budget, forecast, Mircea

---

The respective field has just been introduced (April 17th), it won't affect the rest of the calculations; it is manually filled in as an estimated budget for that forecast, but it isn't processed in anyway. We will soon display the explanatory message for the field 

It is to be filled in before saving the forecast. If left unfilled in, the field should be hidden after saving the forecast.
