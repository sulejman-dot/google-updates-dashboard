# Google Sheets add-on limit

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-05-20
> **Tags:** google sheets, Maria

---

There is no limit of lines when it comes to Keyword data.

When the Group Performance enpoint displays this [https://take.ms/67BtV](https://take.ms/67BtV), you simply add the number of extra lines you need and click Add.

For the Keyword option, it loops until it shows all available results there.
