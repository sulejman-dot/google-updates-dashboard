# What is the expected behavior for the "best rank" label? 

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-04-12
> **Tags:** best rank, Ioana, labels

---

The _All-time best rank_ is the best position ever achieved by a keyword since it started being tracked in SEOmonitor, no matter the timeframe selected in the calendar.

The icon (star representation) is relative to the interval, but it’s still tied to that all-time.
i.e. you could have the star if the rank you see in the table (so for the day or last day of the timeframe in the calendar) equals the all-time best.

Details about the star representation [Rank Tracker - Best Rank Star Conditions](https://app.getguru.com/card/iXAkx74T/Rank-Tracker-Best-Rank-Star-Conditions)
