# ​Why do columns in Keyword and LP view differ?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-01-08
> **Tags:** KW view, LP view, managementslot

---

In Manage:

a. KW view 

- 
the mandatory columns are 3: SERP, SV, Rank  - [http://take.ms/r1l5l](http://take.ms/r1l5l)

- 
in addition, you can choose from among 13 other columns - [http://take.ms/jl1o1](http://take.ms/jl1o1)


b. LP view 

- 
for timeframes shorter than 90 days, we have 11 columns - [http://take.ms/qRPo0](http://take.ms/qRPo0)

- 
for timeframes longer than 90 days, we reduce the number of columns, for loading time optimisation to 6 - [http://take.ms/f3SrBU](http://take.ms/f3SrBU)
