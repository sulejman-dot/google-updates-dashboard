# What is the benefit of doing keyword research in SeoMonitor vs. tools like Aherfs and SEMRush (trying to understand the main differences or if it's just a similar tool and workflow)?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-07-06
> **Tags:** Ahrefs, compare, compare with ahrefs, compare with semrush, comparison, Ioana, keyword research, research, semrush

---

There are more similarities than differences.

But the main differences would be:

1. **Keyword curation **- Like the fact you wouldn’t see the same keyword counted twice in SEOmonitor if multiple pages of the same website rank in the Top 20 or the inclusion of brand keywords which usually influence top stats. 
It removed that extra step that you need to do in Spreadsheets.

1. **Free monthly rank tracking**. After you’ve built a list of keywords, you can track your own rankings every month for unlimited keywords.

1. **Philosophical/business model.** Semrush and Ahrefs could be thought of as a database that monetizes access to the same instance of the data to everyone (hence the costs of multiple users and the 500$ / month fee for using the API). Our philosophy is that once one of our customers acquires data from SEOmonitor, it’s theirs, so we don’t charge access to it. 
API, multiple users, and Looker and Sheets connectors are included in the minimum subscription.

It's probable that both Semrush and Ahrefs have a much more comprehensive dataset than ours, especially in regions that we don’t yet directly cover. 
Some of our customers buy SEOmonitor for Rank Tracking and SEO Forecasting and keep a minimum Ahref subscription as well, so it always depends on the use case.
