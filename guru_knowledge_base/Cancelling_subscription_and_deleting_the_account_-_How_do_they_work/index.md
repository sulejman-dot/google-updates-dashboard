# Cancelling subscription and deleting the account - How do they work?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-10-28
> **Tags:** cancel subscription, delete account, deleted account, Delia, subscription

---

If they decline the Privacy Policy the account DOES NOT get cancelled automatically.

When you delete the account, it should automatically cancel the subscription(s).

If they only cancel the subscription(s) but don't delete the account, they should no longer be charged.
