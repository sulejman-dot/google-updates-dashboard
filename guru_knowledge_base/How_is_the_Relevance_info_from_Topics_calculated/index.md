# How is the Relevance info from Topics calculated?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-04-23
> **Tags:** Maria, relevance, topics

---

The info from the Relevance column, in Topics ([https://take.ms/JqbK2)](https://take.ms/JqbK2) it takes multiple variables into account, including (but not limited to) the ranks for the results (the keywords in the topic), the ranks for the initial query (the topic) and the common landing pages between these.
