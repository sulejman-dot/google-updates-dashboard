# Carbon Emissions Data

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2026-03-26
> **Tags:** carbon, carbon emission, Carbon Emissions Data, Delia, emission

---

**We currently do not track or report carbon emissions.**

Reason:

- We are a small company (~25 employees)
- There is no regulatory requirement for us to report on carbon emissions
- We do not have an internal system/process in place to calculate or verify emissions data

What to communicate to clients:

- We cannot provide official or verified carbon emissions figures
- We can offer alternative data (e.g., number of servers) to support their own estimations, if helpful
