# I enabled the AI Search/Writer add-on. Why is this not reflected in the level of tracked resources from my Billing section?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2025-12-30
> **Tags:** active resources, add-on, billing, Ioana, resources

---

While a plan change triggers a resource update on the spot, enabling an add-on is reflected in the Billing section for your active resources only after the daily update (and if that has already happened, most likely the following day).
