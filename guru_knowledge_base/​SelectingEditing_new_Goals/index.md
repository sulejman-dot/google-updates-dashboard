# ​Selecting/Editing new Goals

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-04-01
> **Tags:** Ana, Delia, goals, organic traffic, settings

---

If a new Goal was set in the GA by the client, it can be selected in the Goals field, in website settings, in maximum 1 hour. 

If you want to change your current goals or delete all of them, the Organic Traffic changes will be shown from the next processing day, onwards. 

Same goes for the estimated additional sessions & conversions ([https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/195466001/8350381583c32cb05c7e1dbc/image.png)](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/195466001/8350381583c32cb05c7e1dbc/image.png), from the keyword's page - they should update (if there is enough data) from the next day onwards.
