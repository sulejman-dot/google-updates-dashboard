# When should Contact Us work?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-10-30


---

If you've logged in with Master pass + still have those cookies, you won't see the Contact us button. Being logged in with [seomonitor.com](http://seomonitor.com/), [bunt.ro](http://bunt.ro/), [studiorock.ro](http://studiorock.ro/) will also remove the Contact Us button.

To test for customers, open an incognito window and login with the hash link from intercom: [https://take.ms/Szmyj](https://take.ms/Szmyj)

If the user has any blocking software (JS Blockers, Ad blocker or DNS filtering on tracking tools) the Contact Us button won't work. In cases where we detect it does not work, they will receive a notification to contact us at mailto:customer.service@seomonitor.com.

**The general rule is that, if we find a FS for the customer he should be able to use Contact Us.**
