# What is the time frame for the metrics in the pop-up from Explore (Keyword Page - Competition tab)?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-01-31
> **Tags:** competition tab, estimated sessions, explore, Ioana, keyword page, ranks, search volume, visibility score

---

[https://take.ms/uwsTY](https://take.ms/uwsTY)

Ranks - weekly (exception - keywords with a very low Search Volume have monthly ranks)

Search Volume - monthly

Visibility Score - monthly

Estimated sessions - monthly

*Monthly and weekly mean they are not older than a month or, respectively, a week ([https://take.ms/EsDYA](https://take.ms/EsDYA), [https://take.ms/JliYP](https://take.ms/JliYP)
