# How are the keywords under the magnifying glass selected?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-06-12
> **Tags:** competition insights, criteria for relevant keywords, magnifying glass, Mircea

---

In Competition Insights, under the magnifying glass, you can see  some keywords, for both you and the competitors.

"These are the top non-branded keywords with a relevant search volume that the site is ranking for."

In order to be displayed there, keywords need to simultaneously meet certain criteria:

- 
search volume over 50 (us/uk) and 20 (ro/tr); for non-DB we must find out from tech guys

- 
rank greater than 99+ (they need to rank on those keywords)

- 
to be non-brand (if one becomes brand, it will no longer be there)
