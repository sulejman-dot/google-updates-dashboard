# When is the billing triggered for traffic tracking?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2025-03-05
> **Tags:** billing, Ioana, M test, traffic add-on, traffic billing

---

Depending on the type of subscription, the billing gets activated for traffic tracking:

- when you connect GSC for the grandfathered subscription (aka Old)
- when you enable the add-on per campaign for the Simple Pricing ones.
