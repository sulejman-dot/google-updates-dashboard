# Export historical ranks from PI Datametrics

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-06-25
> **Tags:** historical data, historical ranks, Katty, PI Datametrics

---

**Step 1: Go to > Position Explorer Table**

**Step 2: Click the option site and select the site URL**

**Step 3: Change the date range based on your preference (past 12 month) and export data**
