# What labels can be filtered with a click?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-07-17
> **Tags:** filters, labels, Mircea

---

Certain labels, if you click on them, filter the keywords in the respective group according to that label's criteria.

For the others, please use the regular filter.

You can filter: seasonal, misspelled, brand of others, low relevance

You cannot filter: brand (yours, green); NEW label ; also valid for SERP features
