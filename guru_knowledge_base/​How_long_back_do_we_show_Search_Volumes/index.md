# ​How long back do we show Search Volumes?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-04-09
> **Tags:** historical search volumes, Mircea, search volume

---

We currently show a max. of 12 month backwards, under Search Trends or under YoY.

Do not promise more, even when customers remember longer available timeframes (that is deprecated).



PS: If we have special requests from the clients, check with the tech team if it's possible to provide this data for longer periods of time.
