# Health Tracker Status Update/Changes

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2020-05-15
> **Tags:** client health tracker, Delia, forecast objective, health tracker, objective status

---

The Health Tracker updates the data every day at 8:00 A.M Romanian time.

This means that if you do some updates to a campaign during the day, such as set or modify an objective, this will be reflected the next day in the Client Health Tracker status.
