# ​How to resurrect any deleted user!

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-07-15
> **Tags:** deleted account, Maria, subscription, user

---

If an owner closes his account (not just cancels it) and decides to reactivate the subscription, once you've reactivated it from Chargebee, you need to also reactivate his email from [https://app.seomonitor.com/smadmin/changeSubscriptions](https://app.seomonitor.com/smadmin/changeSubscriptions).

You type in his email at the above link -> click 'Find' ([https://take.ms/PTmMa](https://take.ms/PTmMa)) and then above all the info, you will see an orange line [here](), with a similar message as this one: 'This user was deleted. Would you like to restore it?' and -> click restore.
