CS helps with the existing processes during the Sales vacation; activities to be split between the CS team members:

- **Activity 1:**
    1. Keep existing deal talks – leads, new client migrations, etc
    1. Take **existing client** requests for calls


- **Activity 2:** 
    1. Take lead demo requests (direct calendar bookings)
      1. **Reactive**, these leads ask for the call
      1. We updated Cris’ 45’ link to the CS team member link (but can make it 45’)

    1. Check (email) replies on these deals in [HS dashboard](https://app.hubspot.com/reports-dashboard/418340/view/9670387) ([these](https://take.ms/nYmBM))


- **Activity 3:**
    1. Process ICP column in the [Sales Pipeline](https://app.hubspot.com/contacts/418340/objects/0-3/views/all/list) ([these, if list view](https://take.ms/k3UZA)) – in the AM
      1. [NEW] Also use “priority” (low, med, high) – for easier planning of daily activities → examples [https://take.ms/jXrQt](https://take.ms/jXrQt) 

    1. **Proactive** → Is it worth pursuing? (3+ SEOs, ok site, big agency, etc.)
      1. Research them, then pitch (with your own 30’/60’ link)
        1. Check their FS - ok experience? If N/OK, report bugs and acknowledge the issues in the pitch. - For example Amplifyed.io, no FS
        1. Check their LinkedIn - do they know us from before? Use that in the pitch. - For example iCrossing, an old user
        1. If there is no relevant info, use a more generic pitch. - For example, mickeyllew.co.za - no site added

      1. Move to Outreach done
