# Does SEOmonitor support the new Analytics generation - GA4?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-03-21
> **Tags:** analytics, Campaign Settings, Delia, ga4, new profile, organic traffic, traffic

---

YES. 

You can connect the GA profile from the Campaign Settings sidebar, the Connected Accounts tab.

If you already have UA connected, and you want to switch to GA4, [here](https://take.ms/Xf5r9) is how.

If you're curious what GA4 is, you can find more details [here](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10089681?hl=en).
