# How do I find out how the Revenue from the Opportunity pop-up was calculated for a certain keyword?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2023-07-14
> **Tags:** calculate revenue, Ioana, Opportunity, revenue opportunity

---

To understand if the Revenue from the Opportunity pop-up is calculated based on the specific keyword data or on the AOV, you have to check its traffic data either in the Traffic section or in Strategy.

If it’s uncovered from _(not provided)_, so it gets assigned traffic data, then it should be specific to the keyword, as we’ve distributed the traffic data into it. If not, it means that the Revenue for that keyword was calculated based on the AOV.

See also [this](https://app.getguru.com/card/ioBdRrpT/How-do-you-calculate-the-Revenuemo-displayed-in-the-Opportunity-popup) related card.
