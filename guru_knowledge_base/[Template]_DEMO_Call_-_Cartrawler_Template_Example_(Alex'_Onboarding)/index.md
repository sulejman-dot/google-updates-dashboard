**Customer Questions during the call:**

**Q1: **How do you know that the connected GSCs are the correct ones**
A: **If multiple GSC profiles are detected, there is a warning in our tool that tells you that you should connect other profiles as well. This can also be checked in Account Setting -> Website settings. Article [Link](https://help.seomonitor.com/articles/885446-how-to-reduce-the-unallocated-traffic-data).

**Q2: **How is the brand traffic identified and how does it know that the unallocated is non-brand?**
A: **Based on the Brand rules set up during the the registration process, our algorithm will filter the words set by you in to brand and non-brand. In cases where some brand words do slip through, you can mark them as brand to have them removed from the non-brand section. Article [Link.](https://help.seomonitor.com/articles/865939-the-brand-non-brand-segmentation-explained)

**Q3: **How can I see in what group my keywords are, move my keywords into other groups and is the same keyword counted multiple times if in different groups (pay wise), add new keywords.**
A: **There are multiple options for seeing in which group the keyword are situated in: Open the specific keyword page and check in the top left (Group Tag), hover over the folder in the Keyword list in campaign view or download a CSV to see all Keywords and all groups. You can move keywords by selecting it in the list and pressing the Move keyword button at the button and we do not charge for word duplication but for each individual keyword. This means you can duplicate words across groups and not be charged for them. Keywords can be added from the Research module via copy paste, automatic search, CSV import or from the Topics platform if you have a subscription (this also applied to the Topics Extension). [Groups Article Link](https://help.seomonitor.com/articles/904771-groups-smart-groups-and-folders) / [Research Keywords Link](https://help.seomonitor.com/articles/935918-researching-and-adding-new-keywords-to-a-campaign)

**Q4: **If I need to track more markets and for different languages, how does that work?**
A: **Unfortunately we cannot have one campaign with different languages and markets. This will require separate campaigns for each individual requirement. [Article Link.](https://help.seomonitor.com/articles/1112815-how-to-configure-campaign-targeting-settings-global-countrywide-or-local-city)

**Q5: **Will I know when Google re-aggregates keywords?**
A: **Whenever there is an aggregation or any significant changes from Google, there will be annotations on the graph to clearly indicate it. There is also the option to click on the square to the right of the keywords for a pop-up listing all aggregated words and also the event column should indicate that changes have occurred for that specific keyword.
