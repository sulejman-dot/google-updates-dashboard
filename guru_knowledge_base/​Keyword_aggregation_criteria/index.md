# ​Keyword aggregation criteria

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-02-27
> **Tags:** aggregation, Ana

---

For keyword aggregation the following are taken into consideration:

- 
Search Volume

- 
Search Trend

- 
Competition OR a "rounding" of the competition, in case the Search Volume was taken in different days for the different keywords (this is a value that comes from AdWords)
