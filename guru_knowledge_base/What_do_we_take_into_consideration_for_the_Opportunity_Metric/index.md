# What do we take into consideration for the Opportunity Metric?

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2021-03-29
> **Tags:** Delia, OPP, Opportunity

---

**The problem:** It’s hard to take the many attributes of a keyword into account when trying to figure out which keywords could generate the biggest impact in an SEO strategy. That leads to either a lot of manual work in spreadsheets or scripts, or simplifying the decision to one attribute, like the search volume. But we all know that’s far from good enough.

**The solution:** One metric that would encapsulate all the qualitative and quantitative attributes of a keyword for an SEO strategy. These are:

1. 
**Search Volume**

1. 
Search Trend (YoY)

1. 
SERP features as % of searches that end up clicking on an organic result

1. 
**Current rank (as in how much better it can get) as [ctr(top3) - ctr(current rank)]**

1. 
Warning labels for weak keywords where the quality parameters become irrelevant (like brands of others, irrelevant keywords, highly localized keywords) as a % reduction in search volume.

1. 
**Difficulty to rank in top 10 as top10(1), easy(2), medium(3), hard(4)**
