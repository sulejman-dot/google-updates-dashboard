# ​Signals - Filters applied to them that are saved

> **Collection:** Customer Success
> **Last Modified:** 2019-01-11
> **Tags:** Ana, signals

---

The Signals cards that were pinned will remain forever saved in the app. 

The date from L@ in mobile will remain saved, and in webapp the link will direct to the Section to the same timeframe and with the applied filters (here there will also be a warning “Signals card filter is ON”).

Worst case scenario, if keywords are deleted, the data will not be complete in the SEO Campaign section, but all details will be kept in the mobile app.
